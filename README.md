## Current Research

### Neural Surrogates for Metalens Modeling
Development and evaluation of data-efficient neural surrogate models for full-wave metalens simulations, including transfer learning, feature engineering, phase representation, and sparse-data adaptation.

### Computer Vision for Biomedical Analysis
Development of computer-vision methods for quantitative analysis of human head, face, hand, and tremor dynamics from video, with applications in objective clinical assessment.
